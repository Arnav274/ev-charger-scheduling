# Road graph validation

Generated by `scripts/validate_road_graph.py` on 2026-09-30. It compares the
fastest routes found by our Dijkstra search (`app/dijkstra.py`) with OSRM, both built from
the same OpenStreetMap snapshot, over 200 random origins in Greater London and
2400 origin-to-station routes of up to 10 km in a straight line.

| Measure | Median | 10th percentile | 90th percentile |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Our distance / OSRM distance | 0.997 | 0.939 | 1.027 |
| Our drive time / OSRM drive time | 0.913 | 0.865 | 0.947 |

- **Same fastest station:** both engines pick the same one of the 12 candidates
  for 85% of origins.
- **Rank agreement:** median Spearman correlation of 0.972 between
  the two engines' orderings of the candidates by drive time.
- **Query time:** 144 ms median, 190 ms at the
  90th percentile, for one origin to 12 stations in pure Python.

## Reading the numbers

Distances agree closely because both engines walk the same streets. Our drive times come out
lower than OSRM's because our model is deliberately simple: free-flow speed on every road,
with no penalty for turning or for traffic signals. That makes absolute times optimistic, but
the ordering of stations, which is all the strategy uses, is what the rank agreement measures.
